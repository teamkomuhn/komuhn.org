Peniche - Treasure Island

We created an artifact to embody a region; an identity holder to cherish and a treasure to share. This project explores how identity, memory, and the sense of belonging emerge, shift, and take root through the stories we tell and the ways we share them. Now on the 2nd edition, this book is the best seller of the region, with over 3000 copies sold.
